## Line 2 — Bounded ZIP extraction and review

**Tried:** SafeZip **44**, ZipAudit **16**, ZipDiff **16** and ZipGate **28** tests; all four capture-based demos; approved extraction and exact audit of freshly captured public API bytes; tree comparison of stored/deflated archives; pre-parser gate inspection and deliberate directory-budget refusal. **Works:** all **8 commands** and integration cases pass. **Broken:** no supported failure observed. Hashes prove byte identity, not publisher identity or execution safety; nested archives stay inert. The line's top-level README is stale at round 24; use ZipGate's current README.

**Goal fit and scope:** general archive handling, distinct from the other goals. Keep the 21-piece goal to stored/deflated ZIPs, portable paths, explicit resource limits and a cooperative workspace. Arbitrary archive formats and hostile local race resistance would make it too broad.

## Line 3 — Exact ZTO allocations

**Tried:** RewardSplit **15**, RewardReceipt **8**, RewardCompare **10**, RewardBatch **11**, RewardWindow **5**, RewardMerge **11**, RewardAudit **13** tests; all seven demos; allocation → independent audit → receipt → batches and verification; budget-window recomputation; cumulative round verification; tampered unit refusal. **Works:** all **14 commands** and integrations matched expected exits. The three-role allocation conserves exactly **1000000000000000000 atomic ZTO units (1 ZTO)**. RewardAudit independently checks quota floors, remainders and the winner/loser boundary without invoking the allocator: the tied final unit goes to `build` by ASCII ID order. Adding a unit to its saved report is refused. Two independent illustrative rounds merge to exactly 3 ZTO. Duplicate-round and largest-remainder budget-reversal warnings remain visible with their documented exit 1.

**Broken:** no supported failure observed. RewardAudit shares validators/rendering/hashing with the allocator; its independent arithmetic is not a wholly independent implementation. RewardMerge totals independently rounded rounds instead of reallocating a pooled budget, and cannot identify every duplicate entitlement. Report hashes are unsigned. Long audit explanations may exceed the 256 KiB output cap and are refused. IDs, weights and supplied addresses establish neither identity, work nor entitlement. Token identity/decimals use the inherited real Ethereum ZTO observation, not a fresh balance query; no recipient was invented.

**Goal fit and scope:** broadly useful allocation/review arithmetic, not cave recordkeeping and not a repetition of planning. A 21-piece line can deliver bounded exact allocation and evidence. Custody, automatic entitlement and actual payment execution should remain outside it.
